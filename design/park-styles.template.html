Park styles

Three softer, rounded takes on the Park tab, each with the same dogs you've spotted in the mockup. Tap a dog to see its name. Leave a comment on the one you like (or the bits you like from each).

1 · Plaza bright and calm

Pale sky, gentle hills and a round paved plaza. Dogs stroll, stop to look at you and hop when they meet.

2 · Meadow sunny and busy

Lollipop trees, flowers, a pond and a picnic blanket where dogs flop down for a rest.

3 · Tiny Planet playful

A little round world that slowly turns, with your dogs trotting around it under twinkling stars.
