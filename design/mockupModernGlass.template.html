Read Me

A modern iPhone app with the feel of Aqua Glass. Big titles, a floating frosted tab bar, rounded cards and full-screen breed pages, with Aqua's glossy blue and the wallpaper keeping it retro.

Tap anything: it works like the app, with 28 sample breeds and some example spots (Progress uses example numbers). Switch screens with the tabs at the top.

To suggest a change, leave a comment on the part you mean, then tell Claude in chat.
