DOGDEX 98 - the Windows 98 version of the Dogdex mockup.

Tap anything: it works like the app, with 28 sample breeds and some example catches. Switch screens with Dex, Progress and Help at the top. Catch a dog, then try the X.

To suggest a change, leave a comment on the part you mean, then tell Claude in chat.
