Dog styles

The Meadow park with four different ways of drawing the dogs, each showing the same dogs you've spotted. Tap a dog to see its name.

1 · Chibi current

Big round head, the same shape for every breed. Breeds differ mainly by colour.

2 · Storybook breed shapes

Side-on dogs with real breed proportions: long low Dachshund, flat-faced Pug, curly Cavoodle.

3 · Stickers big faces

Expressive faces on tiny bodies with a white sticker border.

4 · Pixel matches the 98 look

The Windows 98 pixel dogs, out in the Meadow.
